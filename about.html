About the International Syriac Medical Association

Who we are, what we aim to achieve, how we support the Syriac medical community, and the values that motivate our work.

Who we are

The International Syriac Medical Association (ISMA) is an officially registered association in Austria, Europe. ISMA was established in Vienna in 2010 to bring Suryoye doctors, dentists and other medical professionals together for networking, knowledge exchange, education, professional support and humanitarian service.

Our purpose

Aims

ISMA serves the Syriac community through professional networking, charitable medical work, knowledge sharing and support.

01 · NETWORKING

Networking

Our goal is to facilitate networking between Suryoye medics, using existing community networks and developing cooperation with churches, organizations, congresses and periodical meetings in different countries. Local executive committees may work closely with the Vienna ISMA executive committee.

02 · PARTNERSHIPS

Partnerships

ISMA seeks partnerships with international humanitarian organizations, professional and public institutions, NGOs and other organizations. Cooperation with church institutions, clinics and humanitarian partners can support medical camps and other activities.

03 · MEDICAL ADVICE

Qualified responses

Through a broad network of doctors across medical specialties, members can exchange professional advice and help respond to medical questions from the Suryoye community when specialist knowledge is needed.

Support

Supporting professionals, students and people in need.

01 · MEDICS

Suryoye doctors, dentists and other medics

ISMA helps develop medical activities including medical services, teaching and research through organization and cooperation between members and branches of the association. Medical professionals may also be supported through observership opportunities in well-known departments and universities.

02 · STUDENTS

Suryoye students

ISMA offers support to Suryoye students in different ways, including understanding university systems, recommendation letters and guidance in finding a residency.

03 · HUMANITARIAN

Those in need

One of ISMA's principal objectives is to provide medical help to people in need through church-affiliated hospitals and free medical camps for poor, weak and marginalized people who lack access to qualified care.

Community dialogue

Platform for Discussions

ISMA seeks to provide the community with an opportunity to discuss ethical, moral, medical and spiritual issues when appropriate, including medical ethical dilemmas as they relate to religious beliefs and questions such as end-of-life care.

“The last target is sharing knowledge, advice and guidance both medically and spiritually.”
Values

Religious Motivation

The main objective behind having a Syriac Medical Association is to serve the Lord through different avenues, learning from the Gospel.

ISMA's published religious motivation reflects the association's Christian identity and its commitment to service, compassion and care for people who are suffering.

Psalm 41:1–3

“Blessed is he who considers the poor. God will deliver him in the day of evil. God will preserve him, and keep him alive. He shall be blessed on the earth.”
Our work

Service through medicine and community

In its published statement, ISMA draws inspiration from the healing ministry of Jesus Christ and from the Christian call to care for the sick, vulnerable and marginalized. This motivation is expressed through professional cooperation, medical education, humanitarian assistance, community support and dialogue.
