International Syriac Medical Association

Connecting medical professionals. Serving communities. Building an international network.

ISMA is a non-political, non-profit association established in 2010 in Vienna, Austria, bringing Suryoye Christian doctors and other medical professionals together for networking, knowledge exchange, education and humanitarian support.

ISMA
